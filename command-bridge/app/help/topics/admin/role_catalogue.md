# Position catalogue

The list of ECC and ICS positions: their names, order, who they report to (the chain of command), and whether they can be assigned.

## ICS positions and ECC roles

The catalogue starts with the standard ICS positions. You can also add roles your ECC needs that ICS doesn't name,
such as GIS support, IT support or a municipal liaison.

Keep the two apart:

- **ICS positions** keep their standard names and reporting lines, so your ICS 203 and Incident Action Plan match what
  the ICP and other agencies expect.
- **ECC roles** you add are local support roles. Don't present them as ICS command or general staff positions unless
  they really are one. If a role does the job of an ICS position, use that position instead of a new name.
