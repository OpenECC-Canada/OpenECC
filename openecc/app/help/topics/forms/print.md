# Print

Sends a form to a printer on your network. Nothing has to be installed on your computer.

1. Press **Print** next to **Download PDF** on a form.
2. Pick the **printer** and the number of **copies**. The printer you used last is chosen for you.
3. Press **Print**, then collect it from the printer.

The form is printed exactly as **Download PDF** makes it. **Preview it first** opens that PDF in a new tab.

"Sent to ..." means the printer accepted it. If nothing comes out, check the printer, or tell your SysAdmin.

No **Print** button means printing isn't set up on this server, or it isn't turned on for you. Use
**Download PDF** and print it yourself. Your SysAdmin manages printers under [Printers](help:admin/printers).
