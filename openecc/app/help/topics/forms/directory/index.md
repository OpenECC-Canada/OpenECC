# Agency Directory

Contact details for the organizations and jurisdictions you work with: their 24/7 lines, main phone numbers, email
and websites. Anyone signed in can read it. Your SysAdmin keeps it up to date in [Lookup Lists](help:admin/lookups).

- **Search:** type a name, number or word from the notes.
- **Calling:** on a phone or tablet, tap a number to call it.
- **"check":** shown next to entries whose details haven't been confirmed in the last 12 months (or ever). Confirm
  the number before relying on it, and tell your SysAdmin if it's wrong.

These are **organization** contacts. The people on duty for a particular incident, with their own numbers, are on
the [ICS 205A](help:forms/ics205a/index).
