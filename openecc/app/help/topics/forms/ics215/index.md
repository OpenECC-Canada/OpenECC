# ICS 215 - Operational Planning Worksheet

The ICS 215 is where the Operations Section Chief plans the next operational period. It lists each work assignment,
the resources it needs, what is already on hand and what still has to be ordered. It's usually filled in at the
Tactics Meeting and reviewed at the Planning Meeting.

- **Usually prepared by:** the Operations Section Chief, with the Planning Section Chief.
- **Approved by:** the Planning Section Chief (change this in [Sign-off approvers](help:admin/signoff_settings)).
- **Who uses it:** the assignments become the [ICS 204](help:forms/ics204/index)s. The Safety Officer reviews the
  hazards on the [ICS 215A](help:forms/ics215a/index). Logistics orders what is in "Need".

General steps for signing and printing are in [Working with ICS forms](help:forms/working_with_forms).

## Filling it in

1. **6. Resources (columns):** name up to 12 resource kinds and types, for example "Engine T3", "Crew T2" or
   "Water tender". Press **Save**: the columns then appear in every work assignment.
2. **Work assignments:** for each one, fill in:

| Block | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 3. Branch | The branch, if branches are in use. |
| 4. Division, Group or Other | Where or what the assignment is. |
| 5. Work Assignment and Special Instructions | What has to be done, and anything special. |
| Req. / Have | For each resource column: how many are **required**, and how many are **on hand** already. **Need** is worked out for you (required minus on hand). |
| 7. Overhead Position(s) | Supervisors needed, for example DIVS or STAM. |
| 8. Special Equipment and Supplies | Anything else the assignment needs. |
| 9. Reporting Location | Where the resources report. |
| 10. Requested Arrival Time | When they should be there. |

There is always one empty assignment at the bottom. Fill it in and save, and a new empty one appears. To take one
out, tick **Remove this assignment when saving**.

3. **Totals (11-13)** add up every assignment when you save.
4. **14. Prepared by** fills in with your name and position. Then sign it from the banner at the top.

## Printing

The official form holds 6 assignments. More continue on another copy, and the totals and "Prepared by" are on the
last page. Text too long for its box is shortened with "(cont.)" and printed in full on a continuation sheet.
